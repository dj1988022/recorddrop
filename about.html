About RecordDrop

RecordDrop is a local-first screen recording tool built for people who don't want to lose their work to a failed upload.

We believe your recordings should belong to you first, and the cloud second. That's why every video is saved to your hard drive the instant you stop recording — before it ever touches a server.

Why we built this

Most screen recorders upload first and save later. If your network hiccups, your video is gone. We fixed that by reversing the order: local save first, upload second.

Open source

Full source code is available on GitHub. Self-host with Docker in one command. No vendor lock-in, no subscription required.
