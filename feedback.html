💬 We heard you. Here's what we built.

Real feedback from people who needed a better way to record.

"I hate that my screen recordings are uploaded to someone else's server before I can even save them. I don't know who has access."
Your video hits your disk first. The instant you hit stop, the MP4 is on your hard drive. It never touches our servers unless you explicitly choose to upload it.
"I'm paying $15 a month just to store screen recordings. If I cancel, I lose access to my own videos. That feels wrong."
No subscription. No recurring fees. The web version is free. The desktop client is a one-time purchase. Your files are yours forever.
"I forgot my password and got locked out of my account. All my tutorial videos are gone. I just want to record, not manage another login."
No account needed. Open the page and record. Zero friction. Zero passwords to forget. Your videos live on your device, not behind a login wall.
"I was recording a client demo on hotel WiFi. The upload failed and the recording was lost. 45 minutes of work, gone."
Works offline, saves locally. Record on a plane, in a basement, on bad hotel WiFi. Your file is safe the moment you stop. Upload later when you have a connection — or don't upload at all.
"I cancelled my plan and they told me my videos would be deleted in 30 days. I had to rush-download everything. It was my content, not theirs."
You own your files forever. MP4 format. On your drive. No vendor lock-in. No deletion countdown. We don't have the power to take away what's yours.

Built for people who value their data

💾 Local-First

Your recording hits your hard drive the instant you stop. Network failure? Your file is still safe.

⏱️ No Time Limits

Record as long as you need. No 5-minute cap, no lifetime video limit.

🔒 Privacy by Default

No forced cloud upload. No tracking. No analytics. Just you and your recording.

⚡ Share in One Click

Stop recording → link copied to clipboard → paste anywhere. No login, no waiting.

🔄 Retake Anytime

Mess up? Hit New Recording and start over. No penalty, no extra charge.

🐳 Self-Hosted Option

Deploy with Docker in 30 seconds. Your videos stay on your server. No subscription.
